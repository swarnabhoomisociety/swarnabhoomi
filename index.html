Our story

Life, closer to nature

Swarnabhoomi Society is an exclusive, secluded residential project in Raipur. Homes with spacious bedrooms, kitchens, lobbies, sit-outs and terraces sit among Olympic-length pools, gardens, temples and a bird aviary. The Society brings residents together to look after these shared spaces.

  • Our mission. Well-kept shared spaces and a peaceful, friendly home for every resident.
  • Transparent. Open meetings, clear notices and shared decisions.
  • Inclusive. Events and facilities for all ages and backgrounds.
